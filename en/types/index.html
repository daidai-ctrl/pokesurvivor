PokéSurvivor type chart

The type chart from the main games, but softened for a survivor game (you only have one active move and can't swap Pokémon): super effective deals ×1.6, not very effective ×0.6, and immunities become ×0.3. Two types multiply (capped at ×2.4). It works both ways: your attacks against enemies and theirs against you.

Rows: attacking type · columns: type of the Pokémon being hit.

Weather matters too

  • Rain: Water is boosted and Fire is weakened.
  • Harsh sunlight: Fire is boosted and Water is weakened.
  • Sandstorm: Rock, Ground and Steel are boosted; the rest suffer.
  • Snowfall: Ice is boosted; the rest suffer and slow down.
  • Fog: Ghost, Fairy and Psychic are boosted.
