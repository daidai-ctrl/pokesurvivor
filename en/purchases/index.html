Purchases and pricing

The basic game is free. Digital purchases are optional and there are no subscriptions.

How payment works

  1. Sign in to your PokéSurvivor account.
  2. Open the Gacha screen in the game and select “View purchases and pricing” to review this product.
  3. Follow the instructions for the checkout hosted by Waffo Pancake, which processes the payment and displays available payment methods.
  4. After payment is confirmed, the 10 tickets are credited to the authenticated account used to start the purchase.

There are no recurring charges or automatic renewals. Tickets are digital in-game items: they have no cash value, cannot be transferred or resold, and do not guarantee a particular character. Gacha probabilities are shown in the game.

Refunds
